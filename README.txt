Pint's Quest Book v1.7 - every quest in a zone, opened like a storybook
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone - all quest, map and NPC data is built in.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsQuestBook
  Restart the game.

THE BOOK
  /qb or the minimap button (left-click: the book at your zone, right-click: your quests).
  The cover carries your character's name - "<Name>'s Quest Book" - in your faction's and
  race's colours, with your class motto and the day your story began.
    /qb born MM/DD/YYYY   records the day the character was created, for the cover
                          (e.g. /qb born 8/27/2023 -> "Entered the World of Warcraft on August 27, 2023")
  One chapter per zone, grouped by continent, dungeons, class and professions, and events:
  - Left page: every quest in the zone, chains grouped and indented link by link, coloured
    by difficulty, with completed / in your log / ready to turn in / available marks.
  - Right page: the selected quest - who gives it and where, objectives, XP for your level,
    money, gear choices and reputation. Click a location for a map arrow.
  - Contents: every zone with your completion. A fanfare when you finish a zone.
  - Your quests: the quest log as a spread - share, track or abandon from the book.
  - Holiday quests stay hidden unless the holiday is on.

  Achievements: a chapter of deeds earned on your travels (zones, dungeons, professions, PvP...).
  Rare Spawns: every rare in the zone with level, where it walks and what it drops; map skulls,
  an alert when one is near, and a note when you kill it.

ON YOUR SCREEN
  - Quest tracker with objectives and a colour swatch per quest; replaces the game's tracker.
  - Auto accept and turn-in (hold Shift to stop it; low-level and repeatable quests optional).
  - World map and minimap: quest givers, turn-ins, trainers, vendors, repair, flight masters,
    innkeepers, mailboxes and more - each kind can be switched on or off.
  - The places each objective can be done are shaded on the map in that quest's colour.
  - /qb find <name or service> points an arrow to the nearest one (e.g. /qb find repair).
  - Optional: the quest log key opens the book instead of the game's quest log.

QUEST RECORDER
  Every quest you pick up or hand in is recorded as the game shows it: who gives it and where,
  level, zone, objectives, rewards, money, the XP you got, and who you hand it in to.
  Recorded quests replace the built-in data, so the book matches your realm; quests the book
  didn't have are added, and new zones get their own chapter. They show "Checked in game".

COMMANDS
  /qb                 open or close the book      /qb log        your current quests
  /qb contents        the contents page           /qb deeds      Achievements
  /qb rares           Rare Spawns                 /qb find ...   arrow to an NPC or service
  /qb born MM/DD/YYYY the cover's start date      /qb arrow      put the arrow away
  /qb tracker         tracker on / off            /qb auto       auto accept / turn-in on / off
  /qb options         options                     /qb unhide     bring back quests you hid
  /qb rarealerts      rare alerts on / off
  /qb rec             what the recorder has saved  (/qb rec off, /qb rec on, /qb rec quiet)

  The Dungeon Journal is its own add-on: Pint's Dungeon Journal (/dj).
