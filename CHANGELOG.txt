Pint's Quest Book - Changelog

v1.9.4
- The book closes when a fight starts (with its quest finder and journal), so it's never in the way.

v1.9.3
- Tracker: a + / - beside "Quests" opens every zone or folds them all away.
- Find a quest: type part of a quest's name in the box at the top of the book - every quest your faction and class
  can do, in any zone, lowest level first, with its zone and where you are with it. Click one to read it.
- The book is a little wider, so the buttons along the bottom no longer overlap.
- Tracker: a zone the game lists twice (WoW Forever: Zul'Farrak) shows once, with all its quests under it.
- WoW Forever: fixed an error that could stop the game's own map icons from showing after the book filled in quest
  locations.

v1.9.2
- Share in a fight now says you can't (the game blocks it) instead of causing an error.
- WoW Forever: minimap icons and quest-area shading stay pinned to their spot as you move (they drifted - the
  minimap there covers a different distance than Classic's; the game now says how far).
- WoW Forever: fewer wrong "!" from Classic's quest list - a quest not yet seen in game that's more than 4 levels above
  you stays off the map until you're closer. Right-click a "!" on the minimap (Ctrl-right-click on the world map) to
  mark its quests "not offered here" - off the map for all your characters; /qb rec unhide puts them back. Talking to a
  giver clears out quests they don't offer - now on WoW Forever too, where the game often hides the NPC's name (the
  giver is matched by where you're standing), and the map updates straight away.
- Shift-clicking a quest into chat makes a proper quest link again on WoW Forever (it came out as plain text): the
  game's own link where it gives one, and the quest's real level.
- Party progress messages now say "Pint's Quest Book: 6/6 ..." (they said "Quest Book:").
- Quest links in chat: clicking or hovering one adds where you stand with it - done, in your log, you can take it
  now, too low (and the level it needs), or an earlier quest to do first - and who gives it.

v1.9.1
- Quest "!" and "?" now show on every level of the map: the world map, continents, zones, cities (a zone's map
  now shows the givers in its city too, e.g. Orgrimmar's on Durotar), and caves or inner maps.
- World map icons no longer get in the way of moving around the map: a plain left-click zooms in and right-click
  zooms out, even on an icon. Ctrl-click an icon to open it in the Quest Book, Ctrl-Shift-click for the arrow.
  (Minimap icons still take a plain click.)
- World map "!" and "?" are no longer faded, so they're easy to see on any map. A "?" (ready to turn in) sits on a
  dark round badge with a gold rim like the game's own markers; a "!" (quest to pick up) is just the yellow mark.
- The "!" is coloured by the quest's level, like the quest log: green below your level, yellow around your level,
  orange above it (grey when it no longer gives XP). A giver with several quests shows their easiest one that still
  gives XP. On the world map and the minimap.
- Tracker: zone names are blue, and the zone you're in moves to the top with its name a little bigger.
- Quest areas: the shading is a little darker on the map and the minimap, and hovering it on the minimap or the map tells
  you which quest it's for and what to do there (with your progress). The world map now shades every area the minimap
  does, including ones inside towns and caves (some were missing).

v1.9
- Journal (the "Journal" button in the book, or /qb journal): written for you as you play - levels, quests handed in,
  new spells, boss kills and your first kill of each creature (with Pint's Tips), deaths (and what killed you, where
  the game says), first visits to zones and places, first rare or better finds, reputation ranks, profession
  milestones, joining or leaving a guild. Search it, show one kind at a time, and add your own entries.
- Level-up message: how long you spent at the level, your total played time, and what you gained (health, mana,
  stats, talent points). /qb levelmsg turns it off or on.
- Tasks (the "Tasks" button, or /qb tasks): your own to-do list, each character its own. "Until done", "Item count"
  (e.g. water under 10 - Shift-click an item in) or "Place" (when you're in Orgrimmar). Item and place tasks remind
  you when they come true and come back after you tick them. Active tasks show in a small list you can move.
- Hovering a quest (in the book's list or the tracker) shows its XP at your level, money, reputation and item rewards
  - each item with its icon, quality colour and upgrade % (with Craft Upgrades).
- A quest's rewards no longer go missing after you hand it in (they were read again once the window had closed).
- Quests with no known turn-in now say where to hand them in when the quest's text names the place
  ("Turn in: probably in Thunder Bluff").
- WoW Forever: the map and minimap now put a "!" on every quest giver the book knows, not only the ones already seen
  in game. Givers not seen yet are faded until you meet them; ones found not to offer a quest stay off the map.
  ("Unchecked quests (faded)" in the map icon options turns them off.)

v1.8.11
- WoW Forever: chapters for all the new dungeons (Hall of Thanes, Excavation Site: Wetlands, The Drowned City, Krol'dok
  Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace) and the new zones (Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Mount Hyjal,
  Zephras Isle). Their quests fill in as you meet them in game.

v1.8.10
- After you die, the arrow points the way back to your corpse while you run back as a ghost (and goes back to what it
  was pointing at once you're alive). /qb corpse turns it off or on.

v1.8.9
- Places near a zone's edge go in the right zone: a quest ender in northern Stranglethorn (Hemet Nesingwary) showed
  as Westfall, whose map's corner reaches over it.

v1.8.8
- Tracker: zone names are a size larger.
- Map: townsfolk icons (repair, innkeeper, mailbox, flight master...) show again - they could go missing for the whole
  session if the map was opened soon after logging in.
- New default: reagent vendors are ticked in the map icons. (Your own settings are kept.)

v1.8.7
- Tracker: a quest you need to use an item for (a Toxin Extractor, a torch...) shows that item's icon, framed in gold, just left of the
  tracker - as tall as the quest's name and its first objective line. Click it to use the item; hover for its tooltip. It shows
  its cooldown too.
  (The buttons don't lock the tracker in a fight, so it keeps updating in combat.)
- Tracker: dotted lines above and below each quest and between zones.
- Tracker: a quest's objectives ("0/10 Incendrites") are indented further under its name.

v1.8.6
- World map: a "Map icons" drop-down beside the book button - tick or untick quests, townsfolk, vendors,
  profession trainers, rares and more straight from the map.

v1.8.5
- World map: the ! and ? of every zone now show on the continent maps (Kalimdor, Eastern Kingdoms) too, with
  nearby givers sharing an icon. Option: "Quests on continent maps"; "Low-level quests" hides the grey ones.
- Minimap: an icon right where you're standing fades almost away, so it no longer covers your arrow.

v1.8.4
- Minimap quest-area shading is about half as strong, so the map shows through.

v1.8.3
- Fixed thousands of "maximum number of mask textures" errors from 1.8.2 (the game allows fewer gaps than it
  used): the shading now leaves a gap for you and your nearest party member.

v1.8.2
- Minimap: the quest-area shading leaves a gap around your arrow and your party's dots, so they're no longer hidden
  under it.

v1.8.1
- Tracker: a finished quest folds its objectives away (a + after "(Complete)"); click "(Complete)" to show them again.
- Quest level colours in the book and the tracker now come from the game itself (on WoW Forever: how hard each
  quest is for you, the same check the game's quest log uses), so they match the quest log (on WoW Forever a
  level 17 quest at level 23 is green, not grey). Grey quests hidden by "show low-level quests" follow the same rule.
- WoW Forever: fixed "attempt to compare a secret number value" from the quest arrow. The game can hide your speed,
  position or facing from add-ons (in combat and some places); the arrow now waits ("position hidden right now")
  instead of erroring, and the time-to-arrive goes by how fast the distance is closing when speed is hidden.

v1.8
- Quest arrow: left-click a quest in the tracker and the arrow points to the nearest place its unfinished objectives
  can be done - or to whoever takes it in once it's complete - and moves on by itself as you finish objectives.
  Click the quest again to stop. Alt-click now opens your quest log.
- The arrow shows a time to arrive, worked out from how fast you're actually closing the distance (your speed and the
  way you're heading), and moves with a middle-click drag. The arrow is a little larger, and the quest it's
  pointing to has a clear arrow mark in the tracker.
- Pint's Alts can show map coordinates for trainers and book sellers and point this arrow at them.
- Minimap tooltip: quest objectives are indented under their quest, so only the quest names line up on the left.

v1.7.10
- The tracker's quest count (27/40) now counts every quest in your log, including ones in zones you've folded away.
- Tracker text is brighter, with a crisper shadow and a slightly darker background, so it's easier to read while
  running over bright ground.

v1.7.9
- Wailing Caverns is back in the Dungeons & Raids menu (1.7.8 left it out by mistake).

v1.7.8
- WoW Forever: Ruins of Lordaeron is in the Dungeons & Raids menu, and quests you recorded there before are filed under it.
- WoW Forever: the City of Dalaran dungeon (levels 28-35) and its 13 quests are built in - the Alliance and Horde
  Dalaran Sewers Key chains in order, the other dungeon quests, who gives them and their rewards.

v1.7.7
- WoW Forever: the Ruins of Lordaeron dungeon's 15 quests for both factions are built in - who gives them, where
  you hand them in, the Unending Torment chain in order, and their rewards.
- Quests recorded with a level range now keep the lowest level you can take them at.

v1.7.6
- Tells your group when you finish a quest objective, like Questie: "Quest Book: 1/1 Console Key for [Samophlange]!" in
  party chat while you're in a group. On by default; /qb options to turn it off, or to also say every step (3/8, 4/8...).
  (It stays quiet while Questie is running, so it isn't said twice.)
- When every objective of a quest is done - solo or grouped - "Quest complete: <quest> - ready to turn in" shows across
  the middle of the screen with a sound. Can be turned off in /qb options.
- WoW Forever: a few more quests and quest givers built in.

v1.7.5
- WoW Forever: comes with quests seen in Forever - quests new to Forever, and where many quest givers stand - so the
  book knows them before you've met them. Your own recordings still come first.

v1.7.4
- WoW Forever: no more errors when the game hides a quest giver's name; the quest is still recorded, without the giver.

v1.7.3
- Shift-click a quest in the tracker or the book's quest list to link it in chat.

v1.7.2
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.
- The cover shows your full name on WoW Forever.

v1.7.1
- Tracker: objectives are indented under their quest's title.
- Tracker: a failed quest says (Failed) in red beside its title.
- Deeds: on WoW Forever, the quest-count deeds (Tales Told) only count real quests - the game's hidden tracking
  flags no longer count, and deeds wrongly earned from them are taken back.

v1.7
- New: the quest recorder. Every quest you pick up or hand in is written down as the game shows it - who gives it
  and where, level, zone, objectives, rewards, money, the XP you really got, and who you hand it in to.
- Recorded quests replace the book's built-in data, so the book matches the realm you play on (made for WoW Forever,
  where many quests changed). Quests the book didn't have are added, and new zones get their own chapter.
- Quests checked in game say so in the book and in their tooltip.
- /qb rec shows what's been recorded; /qb rec off pauses it.
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged:
  the quest log, watches and tracker use the modern quest functions; on the map only quests checked by the
  recorder get pins ("Unchecked quests" option shows the rest); rare kills come from the rare dying or its loot,
  and dungeon deeds from boss encounters, since the combat log can't be read there.
- Map pins no longer cause blocked-action errors on the world map.

v1.6
- First public release as Pint's Quest Book.
- The Dungeon Journal is now its own add-on, Pint's Dungeon Journal; /qb journal opens it when installed.
- Holiday quests are hidden unless the holiday is active.
- Map icons skip quests you're too low or too high level for.
