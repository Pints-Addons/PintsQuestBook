Pint's Quest Book - Changelog

v1.9.14
- WoW Forever: Threading a Solution (Kal'tinzan, Mount Hyjal) waits for Blight No More to be done.
- Tracker: the zone you're in goes to the top by the map you're on too, not only the zone's name (Mount Hyjal stayed
  down the list on WoW Forever).
- WoW Forever: Barkskin Burrow's quests (Windswept Wards and the rest in the Burrow) wait until the Burrow is at least
  neutral to you. Reputation requirements are also checked on WoW Forever now - they always passed there before.
- WoW Forever: a character's chronicle, diary, tasks and rare spawns could be filed twice - once under its realm and
  once under a blank one, when the game hadn't named the realm yet at login - so achievements went missing (Rain Mon's
  dungeon bosses). The book now uses the last realm it saw until the game names it, and merges the blank-realm
  records back into the character's own.
- WoW Forever: 65 of Forever's own quests had the wrong faction (Venom Maws in Mount Hyjal is Alliance-only, but showed
  for the Horde too) - now as Wowhead's Forever quest lists have them (ForeverFactions.lua).
- WoW Forever: 147 Classic quests had the wrong faction there - mostly ones the book had open to both that are one
  faction's only, plus a few on the wrong side (Mathrengyl Bearwalker's druid quest is Alliance) - checked against
  Wowhead's Forever quest lists at every level, so quests past level 60's list limit are covered too.

v1.9.13
- Quests you're past aren't offered any more: when a later step of a chain is done or in your log, the earlier step
  (a breadcrumb you skipped, or a first step WoW Forever no longer asks for) leaves the map and shows as passed - A Little
  Luck once you're on Luck Be With You, Trouble in Winterspring once you're past Threat of the Winterfall.
- WoW Forever: 173 of Forever's own quests know the step before them (worked out from who gives and takes them on
  Wowhead) - Crate Expectations waits for Premiere Parts, and so on (ForeverChains.lua).
- Find a quest: clicking a quest in the results turns to its zone's chapter with the quest open (the search clears).

v1.9.12
- Filters: Hide chain steps - leaves out a chain's later steps you can't take yet, so only the step you're on shows. (The
  filter labels are shorter to fit: Other faction, Low-level.)
- Chains: steps you can't take yet (an earlier step comes first) are indented under the step that opens them.
- WoW Forever: the raids are gone from the Dungeons & Raids chapter list (and Prev./Next chapter) until they open there.
- World map: a new tick box, Corrupted flowers (in the Map icons drop-down and Options -> Map icons...), hides the
  "!" of every Corrupted Songflower, Windblossom, Whipper Root and Night Dragon in Felwood. They show by default.
- Quests that ask something of you first are no longer shown as ready to pick up until you have it: a profession
  skill (A Blue Light Bargain wants Blacksmithing 265), a reputation (War at Sea is only for those Gadgetzan dislikes),
  or an earlier step done - for either faction's version of it (Bungle in the Jungle after March of the Silithid). From
  the CMaNGOS Classic database (QuestReqs.lua, 529 quests).

v1.9.11
- World map: on the continent and world maps the quest ! and other icons only show their tooltip - a click goes through
  to the map and opens the zone. On a zone's own map they take clicks as before (Ctrl-click works anywhere).
- WoW Forever: all 1,427 Classic townsfolk were checked against Wowhead's WoW Forever NPC pages - every one is there.
  One that stands more than 30 yards from its Classic spot is shown where Forever has it. Kalldan Felmoon and Waldor
  are confirmed and back on the map.

v1.9.10
- WoW Forever: the Classic rares are where Forever has them - a spot Forever doesn't have one near is dropped, Forever's
  own spots are added, and a rare that moved shows its new home. Their levels and best drops (1% or better) are
  Forever's too. From each rare's Wowhead WoW Forever page.
- WoW Forever: every Classic rare was checked against Wowhead's Forever pages - 334 of 335 are there. Geomancer
  Flintdagger isn't, and two Classic townsfolk (Kalldan Felmoon, Waldor) aren't confirmed yet, so they're hidden until
  they are; /qb classic shows them.
- WoW Forever: 225 townsfolk Forever added are on the map and minimap - the City of Dalaran's innkeepers, bankers,
  trainers and battlemasters, the new class trainers (Shaman in Dun Morogh, Paladin in Tirisfal...), the Junior
  gathering trainers, and new innkeepers, stable and flight masters and vendors. From Wowhead's WoW Forever NPC pages.
- WoW Forever: 81 rares join the Rare Spawns chapter - Zephras Isle's, the Riverglades' (Gul'gash, Scaldaron, Mad
  Marrius...), Mount Hyjal's (Pexmit, Old Rusthowl...), the new starting-zone rares, and Classic ones the list
  missed (Barnabus, Avalanchion...) - with where they've been seen and their best drops.
- WoW Forever: quests copied from the published beta guides no longer override Wowhead's WoW Forever data (78 quests).
- WoW Forever: 456 of Forever's own quests shade where their objectives can be done on the map, like the Classic quests
  - from the maps on Wowhead's WoW Forever quest pages.

v1.9.9
- WoW Forever: Forever's own quests (1,435 of them) know who gives and takes them and where they stand, from Wowhead's
  WoW Forever quest pages - on the map, the arrow and the quest's tooltip before you've ever seen the giver. Quests
  that start from an item have only their turn-in. A giver you've met in game still wins.

v1.9.6
- Quest tracker item buttons sit level with their quest again. After the list changed (a quest added, done or a zone
  folded) they could sit a line or more off, beside the wrong quest or a zone name.
- Quest tracker item buttons only go on quests the item is for. An item now has to share a word of its name, or two
  words of its "Use:" text, with the quest - one shared word (like "crystal") put items on unrelated quests.
- Quest tracker: an item the game names for a quest only shows on that quest (Sign of the Earth showed on two).
- WoW Forever: quest experience is Forever's own, from Wowhead's WoW Forever database (4,768 quests) - it was
  worked out from Classic's rewards, and Forever changed many (Call of Water 185, not 1,250) and its own quests had none.
  XP you actually get in game still takes over once you've seen it.

v1.9.5
- WoW Forever: the quest list checked against Wowhead's WoW Forever database. 3,475 of the book's quests are on Forever
  as they are, and 658 changed - Forever's levels, required levels and titles are used now, and a quest's page says
  what changed (rewards, objectives, quest giver...). Map "!" for confirmed quests are no longer faded. 4 quests Forever
  dropped are hidden, and 1,513 of Forever's own quests are in the book (Riverglades, Mount Hyjal, Zephras Isle,
  Shen'dralas, the new dungeons, Crafting Writs, Camping...) - their quest givers show on the map once you've seen them.
- The book really closes when a fight starts on WoW Forever (it also checks twice a second, since the game doesn't always
  say a fight began - a pet or totem starting it), and its X and Close book buttons work in a fight.
- Rare alerts on WoW Forever: the game no longer lets an add-on target by name, so clicking the banner puts
  "/targetexact <rare>" in the chat box - press Enter to target it (this was showing an "action forbidden" error).

v1.9.4
- The book closes when a fight starts (with its quest finder and journal), so it's never in the way.

v1.9.3
- Tracker: a + / - beside "Quests" opens every zone or folds them all away.
- Find a quest: type part of a quest's name in the box at the top of the book - every quest your faction and class
  can do, in any zone, lowest level first, with its zone and where you are with it. Click one to read it.
- The book is a little wider, so the buttons along the bottom no longer overlap.
- Tracker: a zone the game lists twice (WoW Forever: Zul'Farrak) shows once, with all its quests under it.
- WoW Forever: fixed an error that could stop the game's own map icons from showing after the book filled in quest

v1.9.2
- Share in a fight now says you can't (the game blocks it) instead of causing an error.
- WoW Forever: minimap icons and quest-area shading stay pinned to their spot as you move (they drifted - the
  minimap there covers a different distance than Classic's; the game now says how far).
- WoW Forever: fewer wrong "!" from Classic's quest list - a quest not yet seen in game that's more than 4 levels above
  you stays off the map until you're closer. Right-click a "!" on the minimap (Ctrl-right-click on the world map) to
  mark its quests "not offered here" - off the map for all your characters; /qb rec unhide puts them back. Talking to a
  giver clears out quests they don't offer - now on WoW Forever too, where the game often hides the NPC's name (the
  giver is matched by where you're standing), and the map updates straight away.
- Shift-clicking a quest into chat makes a proper quest link again on WoW Forever (it came out as plain text): the
  game's own link where it gives one, and the quest's real level.
- Party progress messages now say "Pint's Quest Book: 6/6 ..." (they said "Quest Book:").
- Quest links in chat: clicking or hovering one adds where you stand with it - done, in your log, you can take it
  now, too low (and the level it needs), or an earlier quest to do first - and who gives it.

v1.9.1
- Quest "!" and "?" now show on every level of the map: the world map, continents, zones, cities (a zone's map
  now shows the givers in its city too, e.g. Orgrimmar's on Durotar), and caves or inner maps.
- World map icons no longer get in the way of moving around the map: a plain left-click zooms in and right-click
  zooms out, even on an icon. Ctrl-click an icon to open it in the Quest Book, Ctrl-Shift-click for the arrow.
  (Minimap icons still take a plain click.)
- World map "!" and "?" are no longer faded, so they're easy to see on any map. A "?" (ready to turn in) sits on a
  dark round badge with a gold rim like the game's own markers; a "!" (quest to pick up) is just the yellow mark.
- The "!" is coloured by the quest's level, like the quest log: green below your level, yellow around your level,
  orange above it (grey when it no longer gives XP). A giver with several quests shows their easiest one that still
  gives XP. On the world map and the minimap.
- Tracker: zone names are blue, and the zone you're in moves to the top with its name a little bigger.
- Quest areas: the shading is a little darker on the map and the minimap, and hovering it on the minimap or the map tells
  you which quest it's for and what to do there (with your progress). The world map now shades every area the minimap
  does, including ones inside towns and caves (some were missing).

v1.9
- Journal (the "Journal" button in the book, or /qb journal): written for you as you play - levels, quests handed in,
  new spells, boss kills and your first kill of each creature (with Pint's Tips), deaths (and what killed you, where
  the game says), first visits to zones and places, first rare or better finds, reputation ranks, profession
  milestones, joining or leaving a guild. Search it, show one kind at a time, and add your own entries.
- Level-up message: how long you spent at the level, your total played time, and what you gained (health, mana,
  stats, talent points). /qb levelmsg turns it off or on.
- Tasks (the "Tasks" button, or /qb tasks): your own to-do list, each character its own. "Until done", "Item count"
  (e.g. water under 10 - Shift-click an item in) or "Place" (when you're in Orgrimmar). Item and place tasks remind
  you when they come true and come back after you tick them. Active tasks show in a small list you can move.
- Hovering a quest (in the book's list or the tracker) shows its XP at your level, money, reputation and item rewards
  - each item with its icon, quality colour and upgrade % (with Craft Upgrades).
- A quest's rewards no longer go missing after you hand it in (they were read again once the window had closed).
- Quests with no known turn-in now say where to hand them in when the quest's text names the place
  ("Turn in: probably in Thunder Bluff").
- WoW Forever: the map and minimap now put a "!" on every quest giver the book knows, not only the ones already seen
  in game. Givers not seen yet are faded until you meet them; ones found not to offer a quest stay off the map.
  ("Unchecked quests (faded)" in the map icon options turns them off.)

v1.7
- New: the quest recorder. Every quest you pick up or hand in is written down as the game shows it - who gives it
  and where, level, zone, objectives, rewards, money, the XP you really got, and who you hand it in to.
- Recorded quests replace the book's built-in data, so the book matches the realm you play on (made for WoW Forever,
  where many quests changed). Quests the book didn't have are added, and new zones get their own chapter.
- Quests checked in game say so in the book and in their tooltip.
- /qb rec shows what's been recorded; /qb rec off pauses it.
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged:
  the quest log, watches and tracker use the modern quest functions; on the map only quests checked by the
  recorder get pins ("Unchecked quests" option shows the rest); rare kills come from the rare dying or its loot,
  and dungeon deeds from boss encounters, since the combat log can't be read there.
- Map pins no longer cause blocked-action errors on the world map.
